# The Grocery List

```
To: Mom
I still have the grocery list from that Tuesday, the one where you wrote 'breathe' at the bottom instead of milk, and I never told you I found it in my coat pocket three winters after you died.
```

**Why it fits.** It is specific and honest: one Tuesday, one list, one word where the milk should be, one coat pocket. It is locked to its addressee, since only a mother could have written "breathe" on a grocery list, and swapping "Mom" breaks it. The reason it stayed unsent is felt, not stated: the person who could receive it is gone, and the finding came three winters too late. It is one line, typed as it was, with no explanation after it.
